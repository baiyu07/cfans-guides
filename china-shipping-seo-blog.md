### Express couriers: fastest and easiest for small parcels

DHL, UPS, FedEx, and similar networks offer door-to-door service, frequent scans, and customs-broker support. They suit samples, replacement parts, or valuable purchases where speed matters more than the lowest price. Expect higher rates and dimensional-weight charges.

### Air freight: a useful middle ground

Air freight can beat express pricing as shipments grow while remaining much faster than sea. Confirm whether an attractive airport-to-airport quote includes collection, export handling, customs clearance, and final delivery.

### Sea freight: lowest unit cost for genuine volume

Sea freight usually wins for heavy or bulky cargo. Less-than-container load (LCL) shares space and is quoted by cubic metre; full-container load (FCL) reserves a container. Port, documentation, customs, and inland charges can make sea uneconomical for one small parcel.

### ePacket-style budget lines: patient shipping for lighter orders

Tracked postal and special-line services trade speed for price. They suit compact items, but names and limits vary by country. Tracking can be less detailed during handoffs, and restricted-item rules may be tighter.

## Chargeable weight can matter more than scale weight

For air and express services, the carrier commonly compares actual weight with volumetric weight and bills whichever is higher. DHL Express, for example, publishes this formula for measurements in centimetres:

> **Volumetric weight (kg) = length × width × height ÷ 5,000**
> A 50 × 40 × 30 cm box calculates to 12 kg, even if the scale shows only 5 kg. Divisors vary by carrier and service — CFans’ example lines use L×W×H÷6,000 (USA/UK) or ÷8,000 (Germany) — and CFans bills by upward-rounded weight (e.g. 8,526 g becomes 8,600 g), so confirm the live rule before booking.

Soft, bulky goods can therefore be surprisingly expensive. Removing shoe boxes, vacuum-packing suitable textiles, or using a closer-fitting carton may reduce chargeable weight. Remove empty space, not necessary protection.

## Customs begins with an accurate description, value, and classification

Customs charges are separate unless the service explicitly includes duties and taxes. They depend on destination, Harmonized System (HS) code, origin, value, and special tariffs. Never under-declare a parcel: inaccurate data can cause delays, reassessment, penalties, return, or seizure.

### When you ship from China to the USA

U.S. rules changed materially in 2025. U.S. Customs and Border Protection says duty-free de minimis treatment has been suspended, including for low-value shipments, so buyers should not assume a parcel under US$800 is automatically duty-free. Applicable duties, taxes, fees, and broker charges can depend on the product and entry method. Express couriers often handle the customs paperwork and bill the receiver when charges are due.

### When you ship from China to the UK

For Great Britain, GOV.UK states that Value Added Tax (VAT) applies to goods sent from abroad, with collection timing depending on the consignment. Non-excise goods worth £135 or less generally have no customs duty, while goods above £135 may attract duty based on product type and origin. VAT can be calculated on the goods plus postage, packaging, insurance, and duty. Check current GOV.UK guidance before shipping.

> **Before dispatch, confirm:** a precise item description; quantity and value; destination phone and address; whether duty is prepaid or collected on arrival; and whether the line accepts the contents.

### Tracking is a chain of identifiers and scans

Tracking may show a warehouse number, a line-haul number, and then a new last-mile number. Quiet periods are normal during transport, customs, or carrier transfer. If movement stops beyond the line’s normal window, contact the forwarder with the parcel number and last scan.

## China shipping FAQ

### What is the cheapest way to ship from China?

According to CFans’ shipping framework, the cheapest choice depends on the packed parcel, not only the lowest per-kilogram rate. Sea freight is usually cheapest per unit for large cargo, while a tracked budget line may cost less for a small parcel. Compare live CFans door-to-door quotes after consolidation.

### How long does shipping from China take?

According to CFans, premium delivery options are available; example transit windows verified in Sep 2026 are 8–12 days (USA line), 9–12 days (UK line) and 8–13 days (Germany line) — 1000g examples; actual quotes vary ±5–10% and by weight/line. Broader planning ranges are about 2-7 days for express, 5-12 days for air freight, 10-20 days for tracked budget lines, and 25-55 days for sea freight. Customs and remote delivery can extend these windows.

### Is shipping charged by actual weight or box size?

Air and express services commonly use the higher of actual and volumetric weight. Sea freight is often priced by container space or cubic metres. For a useful CFans quote, compare the packed parcel’s weight and dimensions after consolidation.

### Will I pay customs duty when shipping from China?

Possibly. The answer depends on destination, product classification, origin, value, and current law. Ask whether your line is duty-paid or duty-unpaid, then check the destination customs authority before dispatch.
